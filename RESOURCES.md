## Attribution policy

Write explanations from understanding. Link the primary source near the claim.
Do not copy diagrams or lightly rephrase someone else's lesson. Explicitly label
any adapted idea, quote sparingly, and respect its license. API-specific examples
need version and access information. No third-party lesson or diagram is bundled
in this repository; the MIT license covers this repository's original material.
